# 📡 Telecom Customer Churn Dashboard

A dynamic and interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze customer churn, customer demographics, account information, subscribed services, and internet usage patterns within a telecommunications business. The dashboard helps identify customers at risk of churn and uncover the key factors influencing customer retention.

---

## 📌 Short Description / Purpose

The **Telecom Customer Churn Dashboard** provides a comprehensive overview of customer behavior and churn-related patterns. It enables businesses and analysts to understand which customer segments, contract types, payment methods, services, and subscription durations are associated with a higher risk of customer churn.

This dashboard is useful for identifying potential retention opportunities and supporting data-driven decisions aimed at reducing customer churn and improving customer lifetime value.

---

# 🛠️ Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

* 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Main business intelligence and data visualization platform used to create the dashboard.
* 📂 **Power Query** – Used for data cleaning, transformation, and preparation before visualization.
* 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Used to create calculated measures, KPIs, percentages, and analytical insights.
* 🔗 **Data Modeling** – Used to organize customer-related data and enable effective analysis across multiple categories.
* 📈 **Data Visualization** – Interactive charts, KPI cards, bar charts, donut charts, and comparative visuals.
* 📁 **File Format** – `.pbix` for Power BI development and `.png` for dashboard preview/screenshots.

# ✨ Features / Highlights

## 🔴 Business Problem

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges in the telecommunications industry. Losing existing customers can significantly impact revenue and increase the cost of acquiring new customers.

Telecommunication companies need to understand questions such as:

* Which customers are most likely to churn?
* Does churn vary based on gender or demographics?
* Which subscription durations have the highest churn risk?
* Do certain payment methods contribute to higher churn?
* Which contract types have the highest customer retention?
* How do subscribed services affect customer behavior?
* What role does internet service type play in customer churn?
* Which customers should be prioritized for retention strategies?

Analyzing this information manually from raw customer data can be difficult and time-consuming.

---

## 🎯 Goal of the Dashboard

The primary goal of the **Telecom Customer Churn Dashboard** is to provide an easy-to-understand and interactive view of customer churn patterns.

The dashboard helps to:

* Identify the total number of customers currently at risk.
* Monitor the overall number of technical and administrative tickets.
* Analyze customer charges and revenue-related metrics.
* Understand churn distribution across customer demographics.
* Analyze churn based on subscription duration.
* Compare customer payment methods.
* Identify the impact of paperless billing.
* Analyze customer contract types.
* Understand which additional services customers subscribe to.
* Compare different internet service users.
* Support customer retention and churn reduction strategies.
